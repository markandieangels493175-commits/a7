Privacy Policy

Clear information about how Supper Future Line operates.

Information we receive

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, subject and message you provide. We use this information to answer your enquiry and keep an appropriate correspondence record.

Analytics and consent

Google Analytics is configured with consent mode. Analytics storage is denied by default and enabled only if you select “Allow analytics.” Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation remain denied.

We use information to operate and secure the site, respond to enquiries and meet legal duties. We do not sell personal information. Limited service providers may process data under appropriate instructions. Records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary.

Your choices

Declining analytics does not restrict site access. To request access, correction or deletion of information linked to an enquiry, email hello@supperfutureline.com. Legal exceptions may apply.
